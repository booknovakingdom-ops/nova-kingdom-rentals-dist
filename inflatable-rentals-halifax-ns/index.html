Frequently Asked Questions

Are you a Halifax inflatable rental company?
We're based in Bridgewater on the South Shore and deliver to all of Halifax Regional Municipality. Halifax is one of our busiest areas, and our team handles delivery, setup and takedown for every booking.
What inflatables are best for a big Halifax school or community event?
Crown Rush 42 (our 42 ft obstacle course), Crown Safari and Crown Pirate Adventure keep lines moving for big groups. Add Crown Axe Challenge or Crown Kick Darts for teens and adults.
Can you set up inflatables in a school gym?
Yes, many units can go indoors in a gym with enough space and ceiling height. We anchor with sandbags indoors. Tell us the room size when you request a quote.
Which parts of Halifax do you deliver to?
All of Halifax Regional Municipality, including downtown Halifax and the peninsula, Clayton Park, Fairview, Spryfield, Armdale, Bedford, Dartmouth, Cole Harbour, Eastern Passage, Lower Sackville, Fall River, Hammonds Plains, Tantallon, Timberlea and Beaver Bank. Send us your address and we'll quote delivery.
What happens if it's windy on my event day?
Inflatables can't be used in winds of 38 km/h or higher, or during thunder and lightning. Halifax gets coastal wind, so we watch the forecast with you and talk about options before the day.
Can you set up in an HRM park or public space?
Yes, where the Halifax Regional Municipality allows it. For events in HRM parks or public spaces, check with the municipality about park bookings or permits before your event. We are fully insured and can provide our certificate of insurance if they ask for it.
Is my booking confirmed when I send a request?
Not yet. We first confirm availability, delivery and setup details. Your date is held once the deposit is received.
