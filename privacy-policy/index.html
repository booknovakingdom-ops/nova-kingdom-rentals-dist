Privacy Policy

Nova Kingdom Rentals (operated by Baaz Global Inc., Bridgewater, Nova Scotia) respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you use our website or contact us, and how we use it.

Information we collect

When you request a quote, check availability, or contact us through our website forms or lead forms, we collect the details you provide, such as your name, phone number, email address, event date, and event details.

We also use website measurement tools, including Meta Pixel, which collect general information about visits to our website (such as pages viewed) to help us understand interest in our rentals and improve our advertising. This information does not identify you by name unless you have submitted a form.

How we use your information

We use your information to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and provide quotes;
  • Arrange, confirm, and manage your rentals;
  • Communicate with you about your booking.

We do not sell your personal information.

Sharing

We share your information only where needed to run our business — for example, with service providers that help us process payments or deliver services — and where required by law.

Retention

We keep your information for as long as needed for your inquiry or booking and for our business records, and then delete it when it is no longer needed.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete your personal information by contacting us through the contact form at novakingdomrentals.com/contact.

Children's privacy

Our services are arranged by adults organizing events. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page.

Effective date: October 30, 2026
