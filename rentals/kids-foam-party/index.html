Make your next kids' event unforgettable with a Nova Kingdom Rentals foam party. Our foam machine blasts kid-safe, non-toxic foam into a wild foam zone that kids love. Water source and power access required. Outdoor only on a flat, grassy area.

Foam Party Packages (price is for the first hour; extra hours available): • Foam & Go Mini — self-pickup, ~15–20 kids — $299 (+$125 each extra hour) • Foam & Go Max — self-pickup, ~30–40 kids — $499 (+$245 each extra hour) • Kingdom Foam Starter — we deliver, you run it, ~20–25 kids — $399 (+$125 each extra hour) • Royal Foam Party — fully hosted, ~40–50 kids — $799 (+$399 each extra hour) • Kingdom Foam Fest — fully hosted, ~60–80 kids — $1,389 (+$799 each extra hour) • Kingdom Foam Fest Plus — fully hosted, ~90–110 kids — $1,789 (+$995 each extra hour) • Ultimate Kingdom Foam Fest — fully hosted, ~120–160 kids — $2,179 (+$1,189 each extra hour) • Royal Colour Foam add-on — $99 per barrel

Foam & Go (self-pickup): pick up and return in Bridgewater; refundable damage deposit and photo ID required; foam solution included. Kingdom Foam Starter: we deliver and set up, show you how to use it, and you run the foam; foam solution included. Fully hosted packages: foam machine and foam solution, our staff runs the foam, music/speaker, and setup and takedown included.

Kids Foam Party is designed for children's events only — it is not available for adult events or alcohol-serving events. Best for ages 3–14.

Foam Party Options: add the Inflatable Foam Pit — a 13 ft × 13 ft contained foam play area with 3 ft side walls and a 6 ft back wall. Outdoor use only; water and power access required.

High-energy foam fun for kids' birthdays, school fun days, daycares, summer camps, church events, and family celebrations.
