Table Rentals in Halifax NS

Nova Kingdom Rentals rents 6 ft rectangular folding tables for food, gifts, crafts and seating at events across Halifax Regional Municipality. Add tables to any inflatable, booth or package booking, or rent them on their own with a minimum order.

We deliver across all of Halifax Regional Municipality, including downtown Halifax and the peninsula, Clayton Park, Fairview, Spryfield, Armdale, Bedford, Dartmouth, Cole Harbour, Eastern Passage, Lower Sackville, Fall River, Hammonds Plains, Tantallon, Timberlea and Beaver Bank. Delivery is quoted based on your event address — request a quote and we'll confirm availability, delivery and setup details.
