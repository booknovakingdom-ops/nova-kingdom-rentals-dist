Frequently Asked Questions

What does a Kids Foam Party cost in Halifax?
Up to 30 kids is $349/hour, 31–80 kids is $599/hour, and 80+ kids starts from $800/hour. Delivery is quoted based on your address.
What do we need for a foam party?
An outdoor space, a water hose and a power outlet. Kids should wear swimwear or clothes that can get wet, plus water shoes.
Which parts of Halifax do you deliver to?
All of Halifax Regional Municipality, including downtown Halifax and the peninsula, Clayton Park, Fairview, Spryfield, Armdale, Bedford, Dartmouth, Cole Harbour, Eastern Passage, Lower Sackville, Fall River, Hammonds Plains, Tantallon, Timberlea and Beaver Bank. Send us your address and we'll quote delivery.
What happens if it's windy on my event day?
Inflatables can't be used in winds of 38 km/h or higher, or during thunder and lightning. Halifax gets coastal wind, so we watch the forecast with you and talk about options before the day.
Is my booking confirmed when I send a request?
Not yet. We first confirm availability, delivery and setup details. Your date is held once the deposit is received.
